# Closed alpha testing on Google Play
{: .no_toc }

[🇨🇿 Česky]({{ "/closed-alpha/cs/" | relative_url }})

Before mobiCut can be published on Google Play, it has to pass a closed test with real testers.
This page says what you'll need and what to do, step by step.

1. TOC
{:toc}

---

{: .warning }
> **The closed alpha hasn't started yet.** There's nothing to install from Google Play today.
> Once it starts, **I'll notify every tester** with the opt-in link and your code.

## The important bits

{: .important }
> **Stay opted in for 14 days in a row.** Google counts only testers who stay in the test for
> 14 consecutive days. Leaving even for a day restarts the count, so please plan for
> **16–18 days** to be safe. Keep the app installed and open it now and then.

{: .tip }
> **You get Pro for life.** Before testing starts, I'll send you a **promo code for lifetime Pro**.
> It stays on your Google account after the test, also after a reinstall or on a new phone.

## Before the test starts

1. **Send me the e-mail of your Google account**, the one signed in to the Play Store on your
   phone. Only accounts on the list can join.
2. **Prepare a test video:** record **1–2 minutes** of anything on your phone (a walk, your pet,
   a ride). Keep it in the gallery.
3. **Optional: a soundtrack.** Download a free track, for example from
   [Pixabay Music](https://pixabay.com/music/), and keep it on the phone.
4. **Wait for my e-mail.** It has the opt-in link and your lifetime Pro code.

## Join and install

1. **Uninstall the APK version of mobiCut**, if you have it. The Play version is signed
   differently and won't install over it.

2. **On the phone**, open the opt-in link from my e-mail, signed in with the same Google account.
3. Tap **Become a tester**.
4. Tap **download it on Google Play**, then **Install**.
5. Open mobiCut → **⚙ Settings**. It should say **Edition: Free**.

## Test Free first

**Don't redeem the code yet.** Once it's redeemed, the account is Pro for good and there's no
way back to Free. Most users will run Free, so it needs testing too.

1. Create a project from your test video.
2. Try **split**, **delete**, **speed**, a **subtitle**, a **picture** and your **soundtrack**.
3. Check that Pro features show the orange **PRO** badge and are locked, and that **Fade
   out-in** is the only transition.
4. **Export.** The video should have the small **(mc)** logo in the top right corner.

## Redeem the code and test Pro

1. Redeem your code, either way:
   - Open the link `https://play.google.com/redeem?code=YOURCODE` (your code in place of
     `YOURCODE`), or
   - **Play Store** → your profile picture → **Payments & subscriptions** → **Redeem code** →
     type the code → **Redeem**.
2. Open mobiCut → **⚙ Settings**. It should now say **Edition: PRO** and **Pro · lifetime**.
   If not, close mobiCut and open it again.
3. Try the Pro extras: an **Interleave** transition and a **second video** in the project.
4. **Export.** The video should have **no logo**.

## During the test

- **Stay opted in** for 16–18 days. Don't tap **Leave the program** in the Play Store.
- Keep mobiCut installed and use it a few times.
- Found a bug or have an idea? Report it in [GitHub issues](https://github.com/bme327/mobicut-public/issues)
  (how: [Feedback]({{ "/feedback/" | relative_url }})).

Thanks for helping get mobiCut onto Google Play! 🙏
