# Editions: Free and Pro
{: .no_toc }

mobiCut comes in two editions. Both are the same app; Pro unlocks a few extras.

## Free is free forever, and ad-free forever

{: .important }
> **The Free edition will always be free, and it will always be free of ads.**
> No ads in Free, none in Pro, never: not now, not after the Play Store launch, not ever.
> Free is a complete video editor, not a trial. There's no time limit and no nagging.

## What each edition includes

| | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Price | **free, forever** | paid (see [Pro pricing](#pro-pricing)) |
| Ads | **none, never** | **none, never** |
| Split, delete, restore, undo | ✓ | ✓ |
| Speed 0.25×–16× per segment | ✓ | ✓ |
| Texts over the video (position, rotation, fonts, colours, animation) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Text slides (title cards from 7 templates) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Mute a segment | ✓ | ✓ |
| Pictures, with zoom and slide effects | ✓ | ✓ |
| Soundtracks (mix or replace, fades) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Graphics: smileys, arrows, shapes, your own pictures, hand drawing | ✓ | ✓ |
| Several videos in one project | ✓ | ✓ |
| Texts and graphics that follow something: **AI path** | ✓ | ✓ |
| … or a path **drawn by hand** | | ✓ |
| Export to the gallery | ✓ | ✓ |
| Export resolution and frame rate | **up to 1080p, 30 fps** | as filmed, up to **4K and 60 fps** |
| Length of the source video | **up to 1 hour** | no limit |
| Transitions | **Fade out-in**, fade in/out at start and end | all, including **Interleave** (crossfade) |
| Logo in the exported video | **small mobiCut logo, top right** | none (optional [PRO logo]({{ "/operations/export/#pro-logo" | relative_url }})) |

## The Free edition's limits

These are the only differences. Everything else works the same in Free.

- **Source videos up to 1 hour.** The video you start a project from can be at most 60 minutes
  long. A shorter cut of a longer flight needs Pro.
- **A small mobiCut logo** (the app icon and the name) in the top right corner of every exported video. It also shows in
  the preview, so what you see is what you get.
- **Exports up to 1080p at 30 fps.** A video filmed in 4K or at 50/60 fps exports at 1080p and
  30 fps; Pro exports it as filmed. Videos filmed at 1080p/30 fps or less export as they are.
- **Fade out-in is the only transition.** Fades between segments and fade in/out at the start and
  end are included; the **Interleave** crossfade is Pro.
- **Paths drawn by hand are Pro.** The [AI path]({{ "/operations/paths/" | relative_url }}#ai-path)
  is free: circle a glider and a text or graphic follows it. Drawing the path with your finger is Pro.
  [Graphics]({{ "/operations/graphics/" | relative_url }}), texts, text slides, pictures and
  [several videos]({{ "/operations/videos/" | relative_url }}) in a project are all free.

In the app, Pro-only features carry an orange **PRO** badge. In Free they stay visible but
locked; tapping one tells you what it does.

## Try Pro now, in test builds

While mobiCut is in testing, **every Pro feature can be switched on for free**.
Test builds are the APKs from the [installation page]({{ "/installation/" | relative_url }}).

1. On the start screen, tap **⚙ Settings**.
2. Under **Test build: edition**, tap **Pro** (marker 1). Tap **Free** to see the app as Free
   users will.

{% include screen.html name="settings-edition" alt="The Settings sheet with Edition: PRO and the Test build: edition switch set to Pro, with the Privacy policy link and the app version below." %}

Test builds start in **Pro**. The switch only exists in test builds; the Play Store version won't
have it.

{: .note }
> Switching to Free never deletes anything. If a project uses Pro features (for example
> Interleave transitions), they stay saved and come back when you switch to Pro again.

## Getting Pro on Google Play {#pro-pricing}

In the Google Play version, Pro is bought through Google Play. There are two ways:

| Option | For whom |
|---|---|
| **Yearly subscription**, renews automatically, with a **free first week** | You edit regularly through the season |
| **One month**, doesn't renew | You edit occasionally, say after a holiday or a competition |

Prices are shown in the app, in your currency, as Google Play sets them. Testers in the
[closed alpha]({{ "/closed-alpha/" | relative_url }}) get **Pro for life** with a promo code.

### Try Pro free for a week

The yearly subscription starts with **7 days free**, once per Google account:

- Tap a Pro feature or **Upgrade**; while you can still get the trial, the button reads
  **Try Pro free for 7 days**, with the yearly price under it.
- Google Play asks for a payment method but charges nothing for 7 days. Pro unlocks right away.
- **Cancel any time** in Google Play → **Subscriptions**: you keep Pro until the week is over and
  pay nothing. If you don't cancel, the yearly subscription starts on day 8.

mobiCut never nags about it: the trial is offered where Pro is offered anyway (a locked
feature, or **Upgrade** on the welcome screen), and **once** after your first export in Free:
*Like your video? Try Pro free for 7 days*. **Not now** closes it for good. There are no other
pop-ups, reminders or countdowns.

Whatever you choose, the **Free edition stays free and without ads**, and switching back to Free
never deletes anything.
