# mobiCut

**A small video editor for Android, made for quick edits of action clips right on the phone.**

## What mobiCut is for

You come down from a paragliding flight or a ski run with a long video. Most of it is waiting,
walking or drifting; a few moments are the ones you want to share. mobiCut lets you turn that
raw clip into a short video before you even get home:

- **cut it into segments** and throw away the boring parts,
- **speed up** the long stretches (up to 16×) or slow down the best moment (down to 0.25×),
- **add subtitles** to say where and what it is,
- **join the parts with fades** so the cuts look intentional,
- **export** a new video to your gallery, ready to share.

The original video is never changed. mobiCut only remembers your edits and builds a new file when
you export.

## How it works, in one minute

1. Start a **project** from a video in your gallery or on an SD card or USB camera.
2. The whole video is one **segment** on the timeline. **Split** it where something changes.
3. Give each segment what it needs: **delete** it, change its **speed**, add a **subtitle** over it.
4. **Export.** The new video lands in `Movies/MobiCut` in your gallery.

Your edits save automatically, so you can close the app and come back to a project any time.

## Editions

mobiCut will be published on Google Play in two editions: **Free** and **Pro**.

{: .important }
> **No ads. Ever.** Neither edition shows advertising, and mobiCut will never get ads.
> Free is a complete editor with a few limits, not a trial.

| | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Split, delete, speed, subtitles, export | ✓ | ✓ |
| Ads | **none, never** | **none, never** |
| Length of the source video | **up to 1 hour** | no limit |
| Transitions | **Fade out-in** (and fade in/out at start and end) | all, including **Interleave** (crossfade) |
| Logo in the exported video | **small (mc) mobiCut logo, top right** | none |

### Free edition limits

- **Source videos up to 1 hour.** The video you start a project from can be at most 60 minutes
  long. Longer videos need Pro.
- **A small (mc) mobiCut logo** sits in the top right corner of every exported video. You see it
  in the preview too, so there are no surprises.
- **Fade out-in is the only transition.** Fades between segments and fade in/out at the start and
  end are included; the Interleave crossfade is Pro.

Features marked with a **PRO** badge in the app belong to Pro. In Free they are visible but
locked; tapping one tells you what it does.

## Requirements

- Android 10 or newer
- Enough free storage for the exported video (roughly the size of the part you keep)
