# Installation
{: .no_toc }

{: .important }
> **mobiCut is planned to be released on Google Play in {{ site.release_planned }}.**
> Until then there is no public download. Once it's out, you'll install it from the Play Store
> like any other app, and this page will link to it.

## What you'll need

- A phone with **Android 10 or newer**.
- About 70 MB free for the app, plus room for the videos you export.

Want to try it before the release? Join the
[closed alpha on Google Play]({{ "/closed-alpha/" | relative_url }}).

## Updating {#updating}

Google Play updates mobiCut like any other app. **Updates keep all your projects.**

{: .note }
> There's no need to uninstall mobiCut to update it. If you do uninstall, see
> [Uninstalling](#uninstalling) for how to keep your projects.

To see which version you have, open **⚙ Settings** on the start screen; the version is shown at
the bottom (for example *mobiCut {{ site.app_version }}*).

## Uninstalling

Long-press the mobiCut icon → **App info** → **Uninstall**.

- **To keep your projects**, tick **Keep app data** in the uninstall dialog. When you install
  mobiCut again, the projects are back.
- Projects are also saved to your **Google backup**, so they come along to a new phone.
- After a reinstall or on a new phone, a project may say it
  [can't find its video]({{ "/operations/missing-video/" | relative_url }}). Pick the same video
  again and all edits are kept.

Videos you exported stay in `Movies/MobiCut` either way.

Something not working? [Report a bug]({{ "/feedback/" | relative_url }}) with a screenshot of your version and the steps that lead to it.
