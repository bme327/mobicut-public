# Delete and restore segments

Deleting a segment leaves it out of the finished video. Nothing is lost: deleted segments can be
brought back at any time.

{% include screen.html name="delete" alt="A deleted segment, hatched in red and labelled Deleted, with the tool showing Restore and the show-deleted eye switched on." %}

## Delete a segment

1. Tap the segment on the timeline to select it.
2. Tap **🗑 Delete**.

The segment disappears from the timeline and from playback; a short red line marks where it was.
The total length under the preview gets shorter.

## See deleted segments

Tap the **eye** at the bottom left of the timeline (marker 3). Deleted segments appear again,
hatched in red and labelled **Deleted** (marker 1). Tap the eye again to hide them.

## Restore a segment

1. Turn on the eye to show deleted segments.
2. Tap the deleted segment. The Delete tool turns into **Restore** (marker 2).
3. Tap **Restore**.

You can also restore from the segment's ⓘ [details]({{ "/operations/segment-info/" | relative_url }}),
or tap **↶ Undo** right after deleting.

{: .note }
If every segment is deleted, the timeline says *Every segment is deleted. Show deleted to restore.*
