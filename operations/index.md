# Operations

Each page below covers one thing you can do in mobiCut. Start with **Projects** if you're new,
then **The editor** for a tour of the screen.

## The editor at a glance

{% include screen.html name="editor" alt="The editor screen with numbered markers on each area." %}

1. **Project name and video details.** Tap to [rename]({{ "/operations/projects/#rename-a-project" | relative_url }}).
2. **Export**: build the finished video. See [Export]({{ "/operations/export/" | relative_url }}).
3. **Preview.** Shows the video as it will look: deleted parts are skipped, speed, transitions
   texts and graphics are applied. Tap a text or graphic on the video to place, turn and style it. A badge in the corner shows the speed of the selected segment.
4. **Play / pause**, with **to the beginning**, **back 5 s**, **forward 5 s** and **to the end** around it.
   The small times next to them are the position (top) and the length (bottom) of the *finished*
   video, not of the original.
5. **Undo** the last change.
6. **Timeline.** Each block is a segment; orange markers are splits; the white line is the
   playhead. See [The editor]({{ "/operations/editor/" | relative_url }}).
7. **Overlay track.** Blue chips are [texts]({{ "/operations/subtitles/" | relative_url }}), purple ones [graphics]({{ "/operations/graphics/" | relative_url }}): tap one to edit it, drag it to move it in time.
8. **Show deleted, zoom out, scroll, zoom in.**
9. **Tools:** [Split]({{ "/operations/split/" | relative_url }}),
   [Delete / Restore]({{ "/operations/delete/" | relative_url }}),
   [Speed]({{ "/operations/speed/" | relative_url }}),
   [Mute]({{ "/operations/mute/" | relative_url }}),
   [Text]({{ "/operations/subtitles/" | relative_url }}),
   [Graphic]({{ "/operations/graphics/" | relative_url }}) and **Add**.

## Add: slides, pictures, soundtracks, transitions, more videos

**Add** (the last tool) puts more into the project:

{% include screen.html name="add" alt="The Add sheet: Text slide at the pointer, Picture at the pointer, Soundtrack at the pointer, Transitions at every join, and Video from Gallery and Video from files." %}

- [Text slide at the pointer]({{ "/operations/text-slides/" | relative_url }}): a title card from a template, split into the video.
- [Picture at the pointer]({{ "/operations/pictures/" | relative_url }}): a still or moving picture, split into the video.
- [Soundtrack at the pointer]({{ "/operations/soundtracks/" | relative_url }}): music under the video, in its own lane.
- [Transitions at every join]({{ "/operations/transitions/#transitions-at-every-join" | relative_url }}): one transition everywhere, plus fade in and out.
- [Video from Gallery / from files]({{ "/operations/videos/" | relative_url }}): another video at the end of the timeline.
