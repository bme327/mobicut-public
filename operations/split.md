# Split a video into segments

A **segment** is a part of the video that you can delete, speed up or slow down on its own.
A new project is one segment; splitting creates more.

{% include screen.html name="split" alt="A timeline split in two at the playhead, with the Split tool highlighted." %}

## Split

1. Move the playhead to where the cut should be (tap or drag the timeline, or play and pause).
   [Zoom in]({{ "/operations/editor/#zoom-in" | relative_url }}) for a precise spot.
2. Tap **✂ Split** (marker 2).

An orange marker appears at the playhead (marker 1) and the segment becomes two. Split as often
as you like, for example around every part you want to keep.

## Move a split

**Drag the orange marker** left or right. The segments on both sides change length.

## Remove a split

**Long-press the orange marker.** The two segments merge back into one. The merged segment
takes the speed of the one on the left.

{: .tip }
> Typical flow for a flight: split at take-off, at the start of the interesting part, at its
> end and at landing. Then delete or speed up the parts in between.
