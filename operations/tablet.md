# Tablets and landscape

mobiCut is built for editing on a phone held upright, so **on a phone the screen stays in
portrait**: turning the phone sideways doesn't rotate the editor.

On a **tablet** (and an unfolded foldable or a Chromebook) the app turns with the screen. Held
sideways, the editor rearranges itself so the **preview is as big as possible**:

{% include screen.html name="tablet-editor" wide=true alt="The editor on a tablet held sideways: a wide preview on top, the timeline under it, and the controls and tools together in one row at the bottom." %}

1. **Preview** across the full width, using all the height above the timeline.
2. **Timeline**, overlay track and zoom row, as on the phone, just wider: easier to place a split precisely.
3. **Play / pause**, the jump buttons, the times and **↶ Undo**, at the bottom left.
4. **Tools** (Split, Delete, Speed, Mute, Text, Graphic, Add), at the bottom right, so both thumbs reach
   everything while you hold the tablet in both hands.

Everything works exactly as described on the other pages; only the places change.

## Editing a text sideways

When you tap a text or a graphic, its editor opens **beside** the preview instead of under it, so the
video stays big while you type and place it:

{% include screen.html name="tablet-subtitle" wide=true alt="A tablet held sideways while editing a text: the preview with the text box on the left, the Text panel with Text, Look and Motion tabs on the right." %}

1. **Preview**: drag the text to move it, the corners to resize it.
2. **Text panel** with the **Text**, **Look** and **Motion** tabs and **Done**. See
   [Texts]({{ "/operations/subtitles/" | relative_url }}).

Upright, a tablet shows the same layout as a phone.
