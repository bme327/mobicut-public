# Text slides
{: .no_toc }

A full-screen title card between parts of the video: the name of the flight at the start,
*Day 2* in the middle, *Thanks for watching* at the end. mobiCut draws it from a template in your
colours; it becomes a segment of its own, like a [picture]({{ "/operations/pictures/" | relative_url }}).

## Add a text slide

1. Move the playhead to where the slide should go.
2. Tap **Add** → **Text slide at the pointer**.
3. Type the **Title** and, if you like, a **Second line (optional)**.
4. Pick a template, set how long it's **Shown for**, and tap **Insert**.

{% include screen.html name="text-slide" alt="The Text slide sheet: Insert button at the top, a preview of the slide, Title and Second line fields, the Templates and Customize tabs with seven template tiles, and Shown for 4.0 s." %}

The video is split at the playhead and the slide goes in between. A new slide uses the design and duration of your last one.

## Templates

Seven templates: **Classic**, **Gradient**, **Band**, **Cinema**, **Minimal**, **Frame** and
**Split**. Each tile shows your own title in that design. The slide has the shape of the video
(wide for a landscape video, tall for a portrait one), and everything on it scales with it.

## Customize

The **Customize** tab changes the design by hand:

- **Text position** (a 3×3 grid) and **Text size**,
- **Text colour**,
- **Font**, with **Bold**, **Capitals** and **Shadow**,
- **Background**: *Solid*, *Gradient ↓*, *Gradient ↘*, *Radial* or *Split*, with its colours,
- **Decoration**: *None*, *Underline*, *Band*, *Lines* or *Side bar*.

Once you change something by hand the sheet says *Customized. Tap a template to start over from
it.*

## Shown for

**−** / **+** in half-second steps, from 0.5 s to 30 s (4 s to start with). After the slide has
been [split]({{ "/operations/split/" | relative_url }}), its length is fixed and the sheet says so.

## Edit or remove

Tap the **(i)** on the slide's segment to open its
[Segment info]({{ "/operations/segment-info/" | relative_url }}), then **Edit text slide…**.
Change anything and tap **Save**, or **Remove this slide**.

A slide is a segment like any other: it can be [deleted]({{ "/operations/delete/" | relative_url }}),
split, get [transitions]({{ "/operations/transitions/" | relative_url }}) on both sides, and have
[texts]({{ "/operations/subtitles/" | relative_url }}) or graphics over it. Speed doesn't apply to it.

Text slides are free in both editions.
