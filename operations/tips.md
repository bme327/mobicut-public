# Tips
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

---

The first time you reach a button or a part of the screen, mobiCut can point at it with a short
**tip**: the rest of the screen dims, the control gets an orange outline, and a bubble says what it
does.

## What a tip looks like {#what-a-tip-looks-like}

- **Got it** closes the tip. Tapping anywhere else closes it too.
- **Skip all tips** closes it and marks every tip as seen.

Each tip shows **once**. You get at most three in one go (each time you open the app), one at a
time, and never while a sheet or dialog is open, or while you capture a path, draw or export.
Using a feature counts as having seen its tip: if you tap **Split** before its tip shows, it won't.

## Which tips {#which-tips}

| Where | Points at |
|---|---|
| Projects | **New project** |
| The editor | The timeline, **Split**, **Text**, **Export** |
| Editing a text or graphic | The preview (move, resize, turn) and the **Motion** tab |

## New installs and updates {#new-installs-and-updates}

If you install mobiCut new, you get every tip. If you update from a version before tips and
already have projects, you know the basics: you only get tips for features that come later. A
new feature in a later release may come with a tip pointing at its button, alongside the
**What's new** notes.

## Show tips again {#show-tips-again}

**Settings** (the gear on the projects screen) → **Show tips again** brings every tip back, the
basics too. The settings close, and the tips show again as you go.
