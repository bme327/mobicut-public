# Several videos in one project
{: .no_toc }

Put a flight and the landing filmed on another camera into one video: add more videos to the
project's timeline and edit them together. **Pro.**

1. TOC
{:toc}

---

## Add a video

1. Tap **Add**.
2. Tap **Video from Gallery** or **Video from files** (SD card, USB, Downloads, Drive…).

The video goes at the **end** of the timeline and the playhead jumps to its start. From there,
split, delete, speed up and mute its parts like any others.

In Free these two items carry the **PRO** badge; tapping them explains Pro. Free's
[1-hour limit]({{ "/editions/" | relative_url }}) counts all the project's videos together.

## On the timeline

{% include screen.html name="media-timeline" alt="The editor with a picture segment (marker 1), a white join between two videos (marker 2), a muted segment (marker 3) and the soundtrack lane (marker 4)." %}

- A **white line** (marker 2) marks where one video (or [picture]({{ "/operations/pictures/" | relative_url }}),
  marker 1) ends and the next begins. Unlike an orange split marker it can't be dragged or
  removed, since the two sides are different files.
- The round button on it adds a [transition]({{ "/operations/transitions/" | relative_url }}),
  as between any two segments.
- The **ⓘ** [segment details]({{ "/operations/segment-info/" | relative_url }}) show which video a
  segment comes from.

## Remove a whole video

Select any of its segments, tap **ⓘ**, then **Remove this whole video** and confirm. All its
segments go, and the texts on it with them. **Undo** brings it back. The project's last video
can't be removed.

## Export

- The finished video has the **size and frame rate of the first video** on the timeline. Videos
  of another size or orientation are fitted inside that frame, with black bars where the shapes
  differ.
- Everything plays one after another, so the export is as long as the timeline;
  [soundtracks]({{ "/operations/soundtracks/" | relative_url }}) running past its end are cut off.

{: .note }
> A project with several videos opens in Free too (for example after switching the test
> edition), but **Export** is locked until you switch back to Pro. Nothing is lost.

## A video can't be found

If the project's first video is missing, the editor asks you to
[find it again]({{ "/operations/missing-video/" | relative_url }}). If one of the added videos or
pictures is missing, mobiCut says which one can't be opened; its part of the preview stays black.
