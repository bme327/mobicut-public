# Welcome screen
{: .no_toc }

Each time you launch mobiCut it shows a short welcome screen with the logo, the app version and
your edition. After 5 seconds it moves on to your projects by itself.

{% include screen.html name="welcome" alt="The welcome screen: mobiCut logo, a one-line description, author and version, Docs, What's new and Privacy links, Free edition with an Upgrade button, and the Hold… and Straight to app buttons at the bottom." %}

1. **Docs** opens this website; **What's new** opens the notes of the latest release;
   **Privacy** opens the [privacy policy]({{ "/privacy/" | relative_url }}).
2. **Upgrade** shows what [Pro]({{ "/editions/" | relative_url }}) adds. In the Google Play
   version, while your account can still get the free week of Pro, the button reads
   **Try Pro free**. In Pro the screen shows
   the **PRO** badge and *All features unlocked* instead.
3. **Hold…** keeps the welcome screen open (the button then reads **Held**), for example to
   note the version for a bug report.
4. **Straight to app** opens your projects right away. The orange fill and the number count down
   the 5 seconds until it opens them on its own.

Tapping **Docs**, **What's new**, **Privacy** or **Upgrade** also holds the screen, so it's still there when
you come back. When held, tap **Straight to app** to continue.

{: .note }
The welcome screen is skipped when you open a video from another app with **Share → MobiCut**;
the editor opens directly.
