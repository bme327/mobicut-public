# Privacy policy

## Summary

mobiCut is a video editor that works entirely on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads, no
analytics and no tracking. It does not collect personal data, does not send your videos or
projects anywhere, and does not share data with anyone. The app does not even have permission
to use the internet.

## Who we are

mobiCut is developed by Ondřej Spilka (bmesoft), Czech Republic, who is responsible for the app
and this policy. See [Contact](#contact) below.

## What the app accesses and why

**Videos, pictures and audio you choose.** When you start a project or add a picture or
soundtrack, you pick the file with Android's own picker (Gallery or Files). mobiCut gets access
only to the files you pick, not to the rest of your storage. It reads them on the phone to show
the preview, the timeline and file details (length, resolution) and to export your edit.
Nothing is uploaded.

**Your projects.** Your edits (cuts, speeds, texts and other settings), the project name, a
link to the source files, a small thumbnail, and copies of the video stills you take and of
the pictures you use as graphics are saved in the app's private storage on your
phone, so you can continue later. Deleting a project removes it. Uninstalling the app removes
them too, unless you choose to keep the app's data in Android's uninstall dialog.

**Exported files.** Exported videos are saved to *Movies/MobiCut* on your phone. They're your files: mobiCut doesn't send them anywhere, and
they stay when you uninstall the app.

**Settings.** A few app preferences (such as your edition) are stored on the phone.

mobiCut does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, accounts or any other data,
and asks for no runtime permissions. Besides *view network connections* (added by Android's
media library, which can only check whether the phone is online), the app has Google Play's
*billing* permission, used only to ask Google Play whether Pro is unlocked and to open its
payment screen. Neither lets mobiCut connect to the internet or send anything.

## What we collect and share

Nothing. mobiCut has no server, no account, no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or other
third-party data collection, and it does not sell or share any data. Because the app cannot
connect to the internet, no data leaves your phone through mobiCut.

## Services from others

These are not part of mobiCut's own data handling, but you may meet them while using it:

- **Android backup.** If you have turned on backup to your Google Account, Android may include
  mobiCut's projects (not your videos) in that backup so they come back on a new phone. This is done by Android under
  [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); you can turn it off in your
  phone's settings.
- **Google Play.** Installing from Google Play, and buying Pro or starting its free trial there,
  is handled by Google. Payments go through Google Play; mobiCut never sees your payment details and only
  learns from Google Play whether Pro is unlocked on your account. See
  [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Links and feedback.** The *Docs*, *What's new* and *Privacy* buttons on the welcome screen,
  and *Privacy policy* in Settings, open this website on GitHub in your browser.
  Downloading the app from this site, and sending feedback through
  [GitHub issues](https://github.com/bme327/mobicut-public/issues), happens on GitHub under
  [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
  Whatever you write in an issue is public; please don't include personal details.

## Children

mobiCut is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Since mobiCut does not collect or store personal data anywhere except on your own phone, all of
it stays under your control: you can delete projects in the app, delete exported files, or
uninstall the app. If you have any question or request about your data, including your rights
under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), [contact us](#contact).

## Changes

If mobiCut ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version
is released, and the date at the top will change.
